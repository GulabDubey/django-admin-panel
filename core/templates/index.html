FAQ

Questions before your first AI assistant goes live?

How does AI learn my website?

It crawls approved pages, extracts useful content, creates embeddings, and retrieves relevant knowledge before generating an answer.

Can I upload PDFs?

Yes. Business, Professional, and custom setups can include documents alongside website pages.

Can I customize the chatbot?

You can customize colors, welcome text, lead forms, handoff rules, and the pages used for training.

How long does training take?

Most small websites can be trained in minutes. Larger websites depend on page count and document volume.

Does it support multiple languages?

Yes. The assistant can respond to visitors in multiple languages while staying grounded in your knowledge base.
